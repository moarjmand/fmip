# Launch phase tasks (D-459)

Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-27 after the whole-project review (product, architecture,
project management, operations and security, all read-only): phase 15 shrinks to security and
finishing, and this short phase invites 20 to 30 people and measures what they do. Drafted by the
product strategist the same day.

Rows are numbered from T-1952 (T-1949 to T-1951 are taken). Rules for every row: at most about six source files; nothing visible is deployed without real-data screenshots and the maintainer's yes (the web-period deploy policy); no row asks anyone to buy or open an account, it states what is needed; no new tracker, no third-party analytics, no new dependency (CLAUDE.md section 2). The maintainer's own steps are in Persian; names, commands and paths stay English. A test row is done when the person who ran it writes the result in the console checklist's item note (status `done`, or `blocked` with the defect's id) and every defect found is a task row or a finding.

Order: A starts at once (it is the longest wait); D1 to D2 and B open while A runs; C and D3 to D5 close the phase; the invitation goes out only when every "gate" row below is `[x]`.
